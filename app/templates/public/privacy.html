Privacy Policy

At PropVal Insights, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: If you register for an account to save your valuation history, we collect your chosen username, email address, and a hashed version of your password.

Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage data to understand how our tool is being used and to improve the accuracy of our models.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your account information solely to provide you with the personalized features of the service, such as viewing your past property valuations.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others.

3. Data Security

We adopt appropriate data collection, storage, and processing practices and security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information and password stored on our site.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage users to frequently check this page for any changes to stay informed about how we are helping to protect the personal information we collect.
