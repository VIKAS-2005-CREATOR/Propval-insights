How PropVal Insights Works

Four steps turn raw listing data into a Mumbai property valuation.

01

Data Cleaning

Historical listings are loaded and cleaned: rows with missing fields, invalid prices, or duplicates are removed.

02

Preprocessing

Numeric fields are scaled, low-cardinality categories (type, status, age) are one-hot encoded, high-cardinality locations (region) are target-encoded, and skewed prices are log-transformed.

03

Model Selection

Random Forest and Gradient Boosting are compared with 5-fold cross-validation. The winner is scored once on a held-out test set.

04

Retraining

When the dataset is updated, running the training script rebuilds the model and refreshes every metric shown on this site.
